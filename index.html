Why Department Representative?

I have had the privilege of serving as the CSE Second Year Class Representative for the academic year 2024-25, actively contributing to our department's growth and student welfare. Since my first year, I have been deeply involved in departmental events and clubs, including Hallabol and Grooves – Alaipayude, and have played key roles in organizing events like Junior Scientist, Axis, and Think India - Pragyan Sansad.

Balancing my responsibilities as a CR, along with academics and extracurricular engagements, has been a transformative experience. It has sharpened my leadership, teamwork, and organizational skills, reinforcing my ability to foster a friendly, inclusive, and well-structured environment within our department. Moving forward, I am eager to leverage my learnings to introduce new initiatives, enhance student collaboration, and create more opportunities for academic and extracurricular growth.

I strongly believe that a well-connected and proactive student community leads to a more engaging and enriching college experience, and I am committed to contributing towards this vision.
